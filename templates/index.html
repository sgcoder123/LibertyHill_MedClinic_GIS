Methodology

The ACS block-group workflow uses 2024 ACS 5-Year estimates joined to 2024 TIGER/Line block-group boundaries. Derived percentages document the numerator and denominator used, and the healthcare-need score is a transparent relative index rather than a proof of need.

  1. Document each source in the metadata catalog.
  2. Retrieve official ACS estimates and margins of error through the Census API.
  3. Join ACS rows to 2024 TIGER/Line block-group boundaries using GEOID.
  4. Normalize selected healthcare-need indicators to a 0 to 100 relative index.
  5. Compare documented indicators side by side without collapsing them into a single score.
